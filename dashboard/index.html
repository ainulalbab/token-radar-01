Token Radar

The pre-token protocol board: every protocol in the snapshot that records no token, ranked by its implied value.

How the value is worked out

The model is fitted from the snapshot itself. A size curve is fitted on the trading protocols that report a market cap: log10(market cap) = intercept + slope × log10(TVL). Each category then gets its own adjustment, the median of log10(actual / curve) for its trading members, shrunk toward zero when a category has few members. A protocol's implied value is the curve value, times its category adjustment, times a growth multiplier (half its 7-day TVL change, clamped to ±30%) and a maturity multiplier (0.75 to 1.00 by age, up to three years). Growth and age are small priors on purpose: on the trading set neither predicts the residual. No protocol name is ever special-cased. Bridges and canonical bridges are not ranked: their TVL is other protocols' assets passing through, and they issue no token of their own.
